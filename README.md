# Works Lab Website

Static HTML/CSS starter website for Works Lab.

## Deploy
Upload `index.html` and `styles.css` to any static hosting provider.

## Before launch
- Replace the resume product `#` link with your actual checkout/product URL.
- Replace `hello@workslab.in` with your preferred email.
- Add your real product preview/images.
- Connect `workslab.in` or `workslab.co` through your domain DNS settings.
